About

An independent R&D studio, not just an AI vendor.

StottifAI exists because AI adoption keeps failing for the same reason: it starts with the technology instead of the business problem.

Built at the intersection of technology and human potential.

StottifAI is an independent research and development studio exploring how artificial intelligence, automation, and modern technology can solve real-world problems. Our work spans operational software, custom AI applications, business modernization, creative media, filmmaking, local language models, robotics, data analysis, and emerging technologies.

Every project begins with curiosity and a practical challenge, whether that's returning thousands of hours to a business, building intelligent workflows, experimenting with next-generation creative tools, or pushing the boundaries of what's possible with AI. We believe the future belongs to people who are willing to build it, test it, and share what they learn.

We build, test, and explore ideas at the intersection of technology and human potential. From AI-powered business systems and workflow automation to filmmaking, robotics, local language models, software development, and experimental research, our projects are united by one goal: creating technology that is practical, elegant, and actually useful. We're less interested in chasing trends than in understanding how emerging tools can improve the way people work, create, and live.
