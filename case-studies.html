Flagship Case Study

Streamlining Operations: The BK Operations App

We designed and built a fully functional internal operations app for an international Burger King franchise operator, replacing fragmented paper checklists, verbal handoffs, and siloed systems with a single, manager-first digital hub.

The app focuses on real-world restaurant execution: shift readiness, food safety, labor efficiency, and accountability, without adding friction to already fast-paced operations.

  • Daily shift checklists and punch lists tied to operational standards
  • Real-time issue tracking for maintenance, cleanliness, and equipment
  • Manager notes and handoff summaries between shifts
  • Labor awareness tools aligned with productivity targets
  • Training progression visibility for new and developing team members
  • Visual accountability using photos and timestamps
  • Clean dashboard views designed for quick decision-making under pressure
  • Easy translation options for multi-lingual staff
  • Internal AI assistant, built into the dashboard

Design philosophy: Built for speed, clarity, and adoption. Every feature was designed around how managers actually work on the floor, not how corporate systems assume they do.

Outcome: Delivered to the client and in active use across their franchise operations. Specific deployment details are withheld under client confidentiality, but the underlying architecture extends cleanly to multi-unit operations, franchise oversight, and broader QSR brands.
